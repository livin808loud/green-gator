# Gator Dispatch — beta shortlist (10 seats)

**Product:** TAKE / COUNTER / PASS for box-truck owner-operators  
**Price test:** $39/mo solo (ask “would you pay $39 or $59?”)  
**Trial:** 30 days free, no card  
**Support:** 24h email, no phone  
**Do not pitch:** your CSRA shipper pipeline (tires/furniture) — wrong ICP

## ICP
- 16–26ft box or straight truck
- Liftgate common
- Amazon Relay and/or DAT / brokered
- Solo or 2-truck
- Has felt the “took a bad load” tax

## Pitch (30 seconds)
> It’s not another logbook. You punch the offer — loaded, deadhead, rate — and it says TAKE, COUNTER at $X, or PASS using **your** insurance, fuel, and overhead. Built by a 26ft liftgate operator, not a TMS company.

## What you need from each beta
1. Real fixed monthly + MPG (onboarding)
2. 5 real offers run through Dispatch in week 1
3. One sentence: what would make this worth $39

## Out of scope for beta

- Factoring dispute suite
- FMCSA autofill
- Public marketing site / ads
